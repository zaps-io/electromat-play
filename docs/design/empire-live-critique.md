# Zaps Empire vs. Age of Empires — Top 5 Gaps

## 1. Nothing is alive
**Gap:** Yard shots 2 and 3 are identical except two amber arcs. Five sedans sit frozen in a diagonal line, hovering over black void. No crews, no vehicles arriving, no construction motion.
**Why it kills it:** AoE *is* villagers hammering, sheep wandering, idle animations. A still frame reads as an infographic, not a world.
**Fix:** Make the 3 crews real units — pickup trucks that drive from HQ to the pad, workers with hard hats who hammer the scaffold (progress bar over the kit). Cars/semis drive in along a road, pull into a stall, plug in with a cable animation, leave. Add ambient life: dust devils, hawks, flickering lights at night.

## 2. The pad floats in a black void
**Gap:** A 4×4 grid of flat, identical brown tiles on pure black. No ground beyond the pad, no shadows, no props.
**Why it kills it:** AoE terrain is painted, blended, decorated and *continues past your base*. Flat tiles + void = a board game, not a place.
**Fix:** Extend terrain 2–3 screens beyond the buildable pad (desert scrub, saguaros, highway, hills). Blend asphalt/gravel/dirt textures, add drop shadows and AO under every kit, a fenced perimeter that grows as you build, and a day/night cycle with charger glow.

## 3. SaaS dashboard, not a game HUD
**Gap:** ~40% of the screen is dark chrome: thin monospace text, stat tables, a "BUILD ORDER" modal strip, a deploy bar with cards.
**Why it kills it:** AoE's HUD is iconic and physical: resource bar top-left, minimap bottom-left, selection portrait centre, 3×3 build grid bottom-right with hotkey letters.
**Fix:** Adopt that layout literally. Resource bar: 💲cash, ⚡capacity, 👷crews, 📈share with icons. Bottom panel: selected building's portrait, HP/utilisation, queue; build buttons as big iconic tiles (DC, MCS, BESS, L, K) with cost tooltips. Frame it in charcoal with amber/red bevels, not borders. Move the price slider into the selection panel.

## 4. Strategic map has no fog, territory, or readability
**Gap:** Real satellite photo, white text-box labels, tiny rival icons scattered. Ownership is unreadable at a glance.
**Why it kills it:** AoE maps communicate control instantly via player colour, and discovery via fog.
**Fix:** Repaint as a stylised parchment/relief map. Unexplored states under shroud until scouted. Colour-fill territory (red/cyan/amber/violet) around owned cities; city pins become miniature building sprites that grow with investment. Draw transmission lines/highways as "roads"; rival trucks visibly move along them so you *see* a neighbour being claimed.

## 5. No feedback, no threat
**Gap:** Income ticks silently; "Grid STRAINED" is a line of text; rival moves are invisible.
**Why it kills it:** AoE's tension comes from alerts — the horn, "You are under attack!", the minimap ping.
**Fix:** Minimap pings + SFX for rival claims, undercuts and brownouts. Visual states on kits: sparking overloaded chargers, dark stalls when strained, angry honking queues that abandon. Floating "+$20K" on the pad each month, selection circles, confirm-click flags.
